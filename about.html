About AltafMedia Store

AltafMedia Store is an online store for premium PSD templates, made for creators, designers, photographers and small businesses. Our mission is simple: professional-quality designs, at fair prices, the easy way.

🎨 What you get

  • Fully-layered PSD files — every text, shape and effect on its own organized layer.
  • YouTube thumbnails, Instagram posts, flyers, menus, wedding albums, event posters aur branding mockups.
  • Print-ready files (CMYK, 300 DPI where needed) and social-media sizes.

⚡ How delivery works

No account or app download needed. Place your order, pay with JazzCash / Easypaisa / bank transfer / Binance Pay, confirm on WhatsApp — your ZIP files are sent to your WhatsApp as soon as payment is verified.
