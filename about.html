About AltafMedia Store

AltafMedia Store Pakistan ke creators, designers, photographers aur small businesses ke liye premium PSD templates ka online store hai. Hamara maqsad simple hai: professional-quality designs, munasib qeematon par, asaan tareeqe se.

🎨 Kya milta hai?

  • Fully-layered PSD files — har text, shape aur effect alag organized layer par.
  • YouTube thumbnails, Instagram posts, flyers, menus, wedding albums, event posters aur branding mockups.
  • Print-ready files (CMYK, 300 DPI jahan zaroori) aur social-media sizes.

⚡ Delivery kaise hoti hai?

Koi account banane ya app download karne ki zaroorat nahi. Order karein, JazzCash / Easypaisa / bank transfer / Binance Pay se payment bhejein, WhatsApp par confirm karein — payment verify hote hi ZIP files aap ke WhatsApp par bhej di jati hain.

  • Jo preview mein dikhaya gaya hai, wahi files mein milega — no surprises.
